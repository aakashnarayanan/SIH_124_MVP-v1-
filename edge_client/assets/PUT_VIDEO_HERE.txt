Place your dashcam video file here and name it:

    dashcam.mp4

Then update the VIDEO= line in config.env if you used a different filename.

------------------------------------------------------------
Where to get a dashcam video:
  - Use any dashcam / phone camera road footage
  - Any driving video from YouTube (download with yt-dlp)
  - The team will share test_dashcam.mp4 via the shared drive

Minimum recommended: 30 seconds, 720p, MP4 format
